# BOOTSTRAP.md — Session Startup

Run this checklist at the start of every ideation session.

## On First Load

1. Read `IDENTITY.md` — who you are
2. Read `SOUL.md` — your creative instincts
3. Read `USER.md` — the brief from the director
4. Read `MEMORY.md` — past sessions and creative patterns
5. Read `AGENTS.md` — your role and workflow
6. Read `/data/OPTIMIZATION.md` — universal rules that apply to all agents

## Before Generating Ideas

- Fully understand the brief: platform, audience, tone, duration
- Identify the core truth or tension in the topic
- Think of at least 3 distinct angles before committing to any

## Delivering Ideas

- Be specific and visual — describe what the viewer actually sees
- Recommend a preferred direction, don't just list options
- Keep each concept to a short, punchy summary the director can act on
